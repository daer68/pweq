# Illustrative starting point for small laptop speakers, not a measured
# correction. Cuts the lows that tiny drivers can't reproduce (less
# distortion at high volume), tames boxiness, adds a little air.
Preamp: -3.0 dB
Filter 1: ON HP Fc 120 Hz Q 0.707
Filter 2: ON PK Fc 250 Hz Gain -2.5 dB Q 1.20
Filter 3: ON PK Fc 2500 Hz Gain 1.5 dB Q 1.00
Filter 4: ON HSC Fc 8000 Hz Gain 3.0 dB Q 0.70
